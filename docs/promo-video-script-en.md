# KrishiMitra AI — 90-Second Vertical Tutorial / Awareness Video Script (English)

**App:** https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/
**Audience:** Indian farmers — including those not highly familiar with
smartphones or AI technology.
**Language:** **English only** for narration, subtitles and on-screen text.
Very simple English. No Kannada or Hindi.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 1080×1920 · ~90 seconds · 10 scenes.
Suitable for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and farmer
awareness campaigns.

> **Ready-to-watch storyboard:** this exact script plays as an animated
> vertical video inside the app at **`/promo-en`** (button "▶ Watch in
> English" on the landing page), with English narration and subtitles. Use
> this document to film the real video (real farmer + real screen recordings)
> or export the storyboard frames.

---

## Ground rules (data honesty — same as the app)

- Never claim a guaranteed diagnosis or guaranteed yield. Use exactly:
  **"possible problem"**, **"AI-based suggestion"**, **"recommended next
  step"**.
- Scene 7 must carry the fertilizer disclaimer on screen:
  **"Use fertilizer according to soil test results and expert advice."**
- Scene 9 must carry the expert disclaimer:
  **"For serious crop problems, consult a local agriculture expert."**
- Never claim GPS or satellite measures N, P, K or pH — soil values come only
  from a soil test / Soil Health Card.
- Show the real app screens (record the actual web app), not invented UI.

## Voice casting (English narration)

- Clear, warm, moderately slow Indian-English voice (~0.9× pace).
- Short sentences. Pause briefly after each instruction.
- Record each scene as a separate file (`vo-01.mp3` … `vo-10.mp3`), two takes
  each.

## Visual style

- Realistic Indian farmers; authentic rural farmland; maize and other Indian
  crops.
- Clear smartphone demonstrations — big taps, slow zoom on key UI elements.
- Simple animations, easy-to-read English text (high contrast, ≥28 px at
  1080×1920), soft background music under narration.
- Friendly and trustworthy. No exaggerated claims.

## Scene-by-scene storyboard

### Scene 1 — Farmer problem (0:00–0:10, 10 s)

**Visual:** Real Indian farmer standing in a maize field, noticing yellow
leaves and pests. Close-ups: yellowing leaf, insect on the underside, dry soil
crack. Farmer looks at the crop with concern. Slow handheld camera.

**On-screen text:** "Yellow leaves? Pests? Soil questions?" (appears ~2 s in)

**Voiceover:**
"Farmers often have questions about crop diseases, pests, soil, fertilizers
and crop management. KrishiMitra AI can help you get useful agricultural
information using your smartphone."

### Scene 2 — Open KrishiMitra AI (0:10–0:16, 6 s)

**Visual:** Farmer's hands unlock the phone, open Chrome, type
`krishimitraai.freebuff.app`. Cut to a clean screen recording of the landing
page loading (English UI).

**On-screen text (large):** `krishimitraai.freebuff.app`

**Voiceover:** "First, open KrishiMitra AI on your mobile phone."

### Scene 3 — Select your crop (0:16–0:22, 6 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: open **Scan My Crop**, select crop **Maize**.
Zoom-in circle highlight on the tap.

**Voiceover:** "Select the crop you are growing, such as maize."

### Scene 4 — Enter farm information (0:22–0:30, 8 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: location card with **📍 Detect My Location**,
weather auto-filling (28°C, humidity), field area and season fields.
Highlight the "Auto" chips for GPS + weather.

**On-screen text:** "Location and weather fill in automatically"

**Voiceover:** "Enter the available information about your farm, crop and
location."

### Scene 5 — Upload a crop photo (0:30–0:38, 8 s)

**Visual:** Farmer points the phone camera at an affected maize leaf; screen
recording of the in-app photo flow: tap **📸 Take / Upload Photo** → dialog
**📷 Take Photo / 🖼️ Choose from Gallery** → take a clear close-up → preview
shows.

**On-screen text:** "Take a clear photo of the affected plant"

**Voiceover:** "If your crop has a disease or pest problem, take a clear photo
of the affected plant and upload it."

### Scene 6 — AI analysis (0:38–0:50, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the result screen: health status chip, the
"Possible problem" card, the "Recommended next step" list, and the
"AI-based suggestion only" disclaimer. Scroll slowly so each block is readable
for at least 2 seconds.

**On-screen text (as they appear):** "Possible problem" · "AI-based
suggestion" · "Recommended next step"

**Voiceover:** "KrishiMitra AI analyzes the information and provides possible
causes and recommended next steps."

### Scene 7 — Soil and nutrient information (0:50–1:02, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the Soil card: N / P / K / pH tiles (from a
real soil test or Soil Health Card entry), the "Measured / Farmer-provided"
chip, and the expert-advice note. Optionally 1–2 s of a real agri-extension
officer talking with a farmer.

**On-screen text:** "From a soil test / Soil Health Card" + disclaimer
"Use fertilizer according to soil test results and expert advice."

**Voiceover:** "You can also use available soil information such as N, P, K
and pH to better understand your soil and fertilizer requirements."

### Scene 8 — Ask a farming question (1:02–1:12, 10 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the chatbot: farmer types (or taps 🎤 to
speak) "My maize leaves are turning yellow. What could be the reason?" → a
simple AI answer appears, clearly labelled as AI-based guidance with possible
causes.

**Voiceover:** "You can also ask your farming questions in simple language and
get AI-based guidance."

### Scene 9 — Farmer using the app (1:12–1:20, 8 s)

**Visual:** Golden-hour wide shot: healthy green field. The farmer stands
confidently using the smartphone, relaxed smile, hand touching healthy leaves.

**On-screen text:** "Use it as a guide — consult a local agriculture expert
for serious crop problems."

**Voiceover:** "Use the information as a guide and make farming decisions
carefully. For serious crop problems, consult a local agriculture expert."

### Scene 10 — Final call to action (1:20–1:30, 10 s)

**Visual:** Clean card on cream background: 🌾 logo, KrishiMitra AI name, a
smartphone mock showing the website, then the lines below. Hold ≥4 seconds,
end with a soft logo pulse.

**On-screen text:**
- **KrishiMitra AI**
- Your Digital Farming Assistant
- Ask. Analyze. Learn. Farm Smarter.
- Try it now
- https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/

**Final voiceover:** "KrishiMitra AI — your digital assistant for smarter
farming. Try it today."

## Production checklist

- [ ] Record English narration first (10 separate files, two takes each).
- [ ] Screen-record the real app on an Android phone (1080p portrait, slow
      taps, English UI, clean browser chrome).
- [ ] Shoot field footage in good light; avoid faces of minors without
      consent.
- [ ] Edit at **1080×1920 (9:16)**; keep all text inside the middle 80%
      (safe area for Reels/Shorts UI overlays).
- [ ] Burn English subtitles for every narration line (most viewers watch
      muted).
- [ ] Background music: soft acoustic/flute bed, −20 dB under narration.
- [ ] Export H.264 MP4, ≤ 25 MB for WhatsApp sharing; also export a
      1920×1080 landscape version if needed for YouTube.
- [ ] Add an English `.srt` subtitle file for YouTube upload.
