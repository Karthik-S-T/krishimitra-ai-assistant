# KrishiMitra AI — 90-Second Promo / Tutorial Video Script (Kannada)

**App:** https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/
**Audience:** Indian farmers — Karnataka (North Karnataka / Haveri region), rural,
low smartphone confidence.
**Language:** Simple spoken Kannada. No technical jargon.
**Duration:** 60–90 seconds · 10 scenes · total voiceover ≈ 90 s.

> **Ready-to-watch storyboard:** this exact script plays as an animated video
> inside the app at **`/promo`** (button "ವೀಡಿಯೊ ನೋಡಿ" on the landing page),
> with on-screen captions and device voiceover. Use this document to film the
> real video (real farmer + real screen recordings) or export the storyboard
> frames.

---

## Ground rules (data honesty — same as the app)

- Never present AI output as a guarantee. On-screen words: **ಸಂಭಾವ್ಯ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ**,
  **ಶಿಫಾರಸು** (never "ಖಚಿತ").
- Show the expert/extension-officer disclaimer whenever fertilizer or pesticide
  action is discussed.
- Never claim GPS or satellite measures N, P, K or pH — soil values come only
  from a soil test / Soil Health Card.
- Show real app screens (record the actual web app), not invented UI.

## Voice casting

- Male or female farmer-friendly voice, slow pace (~0.9×), warm tone.
- North Karnataka / Haveri accent preferred. Record in a quiet room, phone
  20–30 cm from mouth, or use a lapel mic.
- Read each line twice; pick the calmer take.

## Scene-by-scene storyboard

### Scene 1 — Farmer's problem (0:00–0:10, 10 s)

**Visual:** Real Karnataka farmer in a maize field at mid-morning. Close-ups:
yellowing leaf, small pest on the underside, dry soil crack. Farmer rubs a
leaf between fingers, looks at the sky. Slow handheld camera.

**On-screen text:** none (let the visuals breathe).

**Voiceover:**
"ರೈತರಿಗೆ ಬೆಳೆ ಬೆಳೆಯುವಾಗ ಮಣ್ಣು, ಗೊಬ್ಬರ, ರೋಗ ಮತ್ತು ಕೀಟಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಹಲವಾರು ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗಳು
ಬರುತ್ತವೆ. ಇಂತಹ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮೊಬೈಲ್‌ನಲ್ಲೇ ಕೃಷಿ ಸಹಾಯ ಪಡೆಯಲು KrishiMitra AI
ಬಳಸಿ."

### Scene 2 — Open the app (0:10–0:16, 6 s)

**Visual:** Farmer's hands unlock the phone, open Chrome, type
`krishimitraai.freebuff.app`. Cut to clean screen recording of the landing page
loading (Kannada selected).

**On-screen text (large, bottom third):** `krishimitraai.freebuff.app`

**Voiceover:** "ಮೊದಲು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮೊಬೈಲ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ KrishiMitra AI ಆಪ್ ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ."

### Scene 3 — Select crop (0:16–0:22, 6 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: tap **Scan My Crop / ಫೋಟೋ ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಣೆ**, select
crop **ಮೆಕ್ಕೆಜೋಳ (Maize)**. Zoom-in circle highlight on the tap.

**Voiceover:** "ನೀವು ಬೆಳೆಯುತ್ತಿರುವ ಬೆಳೆಯನ್ನು ಆಯ್ಕೆಮಾಡಿ. ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗೆ ಮೆಕ್ಕೆಜೋಳ."

### Scene 4 — Enter farm information (0:22–0:30, 8 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: location card with **📍 Detect My Location**,
weather auto-filling (28°C, humidity), area and season fields. Highlight the
"auto" chips for GPS + weather.

**Voiceover:** "ನಿಮ್ಮ ಜಮೀನಿನ ಸ್ಥಳ, ಬೆಳೆ ಮತ್ತು ಕೇಳಲಾಗುವ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು ನಮೂದಿಸಿ."

**On-screen text:** "📍 ಸ್ಥಳ ಮತ್ತು 🌦️ ಹವಾಮಾನ ತಾನಾಗಿಯೇ ಬರುತ್ತದೆ"

### Scene 5 — Take / choose a leaf photo (0:30–0:38, 8 s)

**Visual:** Farmer points camera at an affected maize leaf; screen recording of
the in-app photo flow: tap **📸 Take / Upload Photo** → dialog **📷 Take Photo /
🖼️ Choose from Gallery** → take a clear close-up → preview shows.

**Voiceover:** "ಬೆಳೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ರೋಗ ಅಥವಾ ಕೀಟದ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ ಇದ್ದರೆ, ಎಲೆಯ ಅಥವಾ ಬೆಳೆಯ ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟವಾದ
ಫೋಟೋ ತೆಗೆದು ಆಪ್‌ಗೆ ನೀಡಿ."

**On-screen text:** "ಎಲೆಯ ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟ ಫೋಟೋ ತೆಗ್ಗಿ"

### Scene 6 — AI analysis (0:38–0:50, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the result screen: health status chip,
"ಸಂಭಾವ್ಯ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ" card, "ಶಿಫಾರಸು — ಮುಂದಿನ ಕ್ರಮ" list, and the disclaimer line.
Slow scroll so each block is readable ≥2 s.

**On-screen text:** "ಸಂಭಾವ್ಯ ಸಮಸ್ಯೆ" and "ಶಿಫಾರಸು" as they appear.

**Voiceover:** "KrishiMitra AI ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು ವಿಶ್ಲೇಷಿಸಿ, ಸಮಸ್ಯೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ
ಸರಳವಾಗಿ ತಿಳಿಸುತ್ತದೆ ಮತ್ತು ಮುಂದಿನ ಕ್ರಮಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶನ ನೀಡುತ್ತದೆ."

### Scene 7 — Soil & fertilizer guidance (0:50–1:02, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the Soil & Rainfall card: N / P / K / pH tiles
(from a real soil test or Soil Health Card entry), the "Measured /
Farmer-provided" chip, and the expert-advice note. Then 1–2 s of a real
agri-extension officer talking with a farmer (stock or local shoot).

**On-screen text:** "ಮಣ್ಣಿನ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯಿಂದ ಮಾತ್ರ" + "ತಜ್ಞರ ಸಲಹೆ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ"

**Voiceover:** "ಮಣ್ಣಿನ ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಲಭ್ಯವಿದ್ದರೆ, N, P, K ಮತ್ತು pH ಮುಂತಾದ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು
ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳಲು ಸಹಾಯ ಪಡೆಯಬಹುದು. ಗೊಬ್ಬರ ಬಳಸುವ ಮೊದಲು ಸ್ಥಳೀಯ ಕೃಷಿ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳ ಅಥವಾ
ತಜ್ಞರ ಸಲಹೆಯನ್ನೂ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ."

### Scene 8 — Ask AI (1:02–1:12, 10 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the chatbot: farmer types (or taps 🎤 to speak)
"ನನ್ನ ಮೆಕ್ಕೆಜೋಳದ ಎಲೆಗಳು ಹಳದಿಯಾಗಿವೆ. ಏನು ಮಾಡಬೇಕು?" → simple Kannada answer appears,
clearly labelled as probable causes / suggestions.

**Voiceover:** "ನಿಮಗೆ ಯಾವುದೇ ಕೃಷಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ ಇದ್ದರೆ, ಸರಳವಾಗಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಯನ್ನು ಕೇಳಿ."

### Scene 9 — Farmer success (1:12–1:20, 8 s)

**Visual:** Golden-hour drone or wide shot: healthy green field, farmer walking
with family, hand touching healthy leaves, relaxed smile.

**Voiceover:** "ಸರಿಯಾದ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು ಸರಿಯಾದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಪಡೆದು, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಕೃಷಿ

### Scene 10 — Call to action (1:20–1:30, 10 s)

**Visual:** Clean card on cream background: 🌾 logo, app name, the four lines
below, hold ≥4 s. End with a soft logo pulse.

**On-screen text:**
- ಕೃಷಿಗಾಗಿ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಡಿಜಿಟಲ್ ಸಹಾಯಕ
- **KrishiMitra AI**
- ಈಗಲೇ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನಿಸಿ
- https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/

**Final voiceover:** "KrishiMitra AI — ನಿಮ್ಮ ಕೃಷಿ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗಳಿಗೆ ಡಿಜಿಟಲ್ ಸಹಾಯ."

## Production checklist

- [ ] Record voiceover first (each scene as a separate file `vo-01.mp3` … `vo-10.mp3`).
- [ ] Screen-record the real app on an Android phone (1080p, slow taps, Kannada UI).
- [ ] Shoot field footage in good light; avoid faces of minors without consent.
- [ ] Edit at 1080×1920 (WhatsApp/Status) **and** 1920×1080 (YouTube) versions.
- [ ] Burn Kannada captions for every voiceover line (many farmers watch muted).
- [ ] Background score: soft flute/tabla, −20 dB under voice.
- [ ] Export H.264 MP4, ≤ 25 MB for WhatsApp sharing.
- [ ] Add subtitle file (`.srt`) in Kannada + English for YouTube upload.
