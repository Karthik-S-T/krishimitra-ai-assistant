# KrishiMitra AI — 90-Second Vertical Tutorial / Awareness Video Script (Hindi)

**App:** https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/
**Audience:** Indian farmers — especially rural areas with limited smartphone
and AI experience.
**Language:** **Hindi only** for narration, subtitles and on-screen text.
Only the brand name "KrishiMitra AI" and the website address stay in Latin
script.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 1080×1920 · ~90 seconds · 10 scenes.
Suitable for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and farmer
awareness campaigns.

> **Ready-to-watch storyboard:** this exact script plays as an animated
> vertical video inside the app at **`/promo-hi`** (button "▶ हिंदी में देखें"
> on the landing page), with Hindi narration and subtitles. Use this document
> to film the real video (real farmer + real screen recordings) or export the
> storyboard frames.

---

## Ground rules (data honesty — same as the app)

- Never claim 100% accuracy or a guaranteed diagnosis. Use exactly:
  **"संभावित समस्या"**, **"AI आधारित सुझाव"**.
- Scene 7 must carry the fertilizer disclaimer on screen:
  **"खाद का उपयोग मिट्टी की जांच और कृषि विशेषज्ञ की सलाह के अनुसार करें।"**
- Scene 9 must carry the expert disclaimer:
  **"गंभीर समस्या होने पर स्थानीय कृषि अधिकारी या विशेषज्ञ से सलाह लें।"**
- Never claim GPS or satellite measures N, P, K or pH — soil values come only
  from a soil test / Soil Health Card (मृदा स्वास्थ्य कार्ड).
- Show the real app screens (record the actual web app), not invented UI.

## Voice casting (Hindi narration)

- Clear, warm, moderately slow Hindi voice (~0.9× pace), farmer-friendly tone.
- Short sentences. Pause briefly after each instruction.
- Record each scene as a separate file (`vo-01.mp3` … `vo-10.mp3`), two takes
  each.

## Visual style

- Realistic Indian farmers; authentic village and farmland; maize and other
  Indian crops.
- Clear smartphone demonstrations — big taps, slow zoom on key UI elements.
- Simple animations; **large readable Hindi subtitles** (high contrast,
  ≥28 px at 1080×1920), soft background music under narration.
- Friendly and trustworthy. No exaggerated promises, no guaranteed yield,
  no guaranteed disease diagnosis.

## Scene-by-scene storyboard

### Scene 1 — किसान की समस्या (0:00–0:10, 10 s)

**Visual:** Real Indian farmer standing in a maize field, looking at yellow
leaves and pests. Close-ups: yellowing leaf, insect on the underside, dry soil
crack. Farmer looks concerned. Slow handheld camera.

**On-screen text:** "पीली पत्तियां? कीट? मिट्टी के सवाल?" (~2 s in)

**Voiceover:**
"खेती करते समय किसानों को फसल की बीमारी, कीट, मिट्टी और खाद के बारे में कई
सवाल होते हैं। KrishiMitra AI आपके मोबाइल पर कृषि संबंधी जानकारी प्राप्त करने
में आपकी मदद कर सकता है।"

### Scene 2 — ऐप खोलें (0:10–0:16, 6 s)

**Visual:** Farmer's hands unlock the phone, open Chrome, type
`krishimitraai.freebuff.app`. Cut to a clean screen recording of the landing
page loading.

**On-screen text (large):** `https://krishimitraai.freebuff.app/`

**Voiceover:** "सबसे पहले अपने मोबाइल में KrishiMitra AI खोलें।"

### Scene 3 — फसल चुनें (0:16–0:22, 6 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: open the app, select crop **मक्का (Maize)**.
Zoom-in circle highlight on the tap.

**Voiceover:** "अपनी फसल चुनें। उदाहरण के लिए, मक्का।"

### Scene 4 — खेत की जानकारी दें (0:22–0:30, 8 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording: location card with **📍 Detect My Location**,
weather auto-filling (28°C, humidity), field area and season fields.
Highlight the "Auto / ऑटो" chips for GPS + weather.

**On-screen text:** "स्थान और मौसम अपने आप भर जाता है"

**Voiceover:** "अपनी फसल, खेत और स्थान से जुड़ी उपलब्ध जानकारी दर्ज करें।"

### Scene 5 — फसल की फोटो लें (0:30–0:38, 8 s)

**Visual:** Farmer points the phone camera at an affected maize leaf; screen
recording of the in-app photo flow: tap **📸 Take / Upload Photo** → dialog
**📷 Take Photo / 🖼️ Choose from Gallery** → take a clear close-up → preview
shows.

**On-screen text:** "प्रभावित पत्ते की साफ फोटो लें"

**Voiceover:** "अगर आपकी फसल में बीमारी या कीट की समस्या है, तो प्रभावित पौधे
या पत्ते की साफ फोटो लेकर ऐप में अपलोड करें।"

### Scene 6 — AI विश्लेषण (0:38–0:50, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the result screen: health status chip, the
"संभावित समस्या" card, the "आगे क्या करें" list, and the
"AI आधारित सुझाव" disclaimer. Scroll slowly so each block is readable for at
least 2 seconds.

**On-screen text (as they appear):** "संभावित समस्या" · "AI आधारित सुझाव" ·
"आगे क्या करें"

**Voiceover:** "KrishiMitra AI आपकी जानकारी का विश्लेषण करके संभावित समस्या
और आगे क्या करना चाहिए, इसकी जानकारी देता है।"

### Scene 7 — मिट्टी और पोषक तत्व (0:50–1:02, 12 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the Soil card: N / P / K / pH tiles (from a
real soil test or Soil Health Card entry), the "Measured / Farmer-provided"
chip, and the expert-advice note. Optionally 1–2 s of a real agri-extension

**On-screen text:** "मिट्टी की जांच / मृदा स्वास्थ्य कार्ड से" + disclaimer
"खाद का उपयोग मिट्टी की जांच और कृषि विशेषज्ञ की सलाह के अनुसार करें।"

**Voiceover:** "अगर मिट्टी की जानकारी उपलब्ध है, तो N, P, K और pH जैसी जानकारी

### Scene 8 — अपना सवाल पूछें (1:02–1:12, 10 s)

**Visual:** Screen recording of the chatbot: farmer types (or taps 🎤 to
speak) "मेरी मक्के की पत्तियां पीली हो रही हैं। इसका कारण क्या हो सकता है?" →

**Voiceover:** "आप अपने खेती से जुड़े सवाल भी सरल भाषा में पूछ सकते हैं और AI

### Scene 9 — किसान का उपयोग (1:12–1:20, 8 s)

**Visual:** Golden-hour wide shot: healthy green field. The farmer stands
confidently using the smartphone, relaxed smile, hand touching healthy leaves.

**On-screen text:** "गंभीर समस्या होने पर स्थानीय कृषि अधिकारी या विशेषज्ञ से

**Voiceover:** "मिली जानकारी को मार्गदर्शन के रूप में उपयोग करें। गंभीर समस्या

**Visual:** Clean card on cream background: 🌾 logo, KrishiMitra AI name, a

**On-screen text:**

- आपका डिजिटल कृषि सहायक

आज ही इस्तेमाल करें।"

## Production checklist

- [ ] Record Hindi narration first (10 separate files, two takes each).
- [ ] Screen-record the real app on an Android phone (1080p portrait, slow
      taps, clean browser chrome).
- [ ] Shoot field/village footage in good light; avoid faces of minors
      without consent.
- [ ] Edit at **1080×1920 (9:16)**; keep all text inside the middle 80%
      (safe area for Reels/Shorts UI overlays).
- [ ] Burn **large Hindi subtitles** for every narration line (most viewers
      watch muted).
- [ ] Background music: soft acoustic/flute bed, −20 dB under narration.
- [ ] Export H.264 MP4, ≤ 25 MB for WhatsApp sharing; also export a
      1920×1080 landscape version if needed for YouTube.
- [ ] Add a Hindi `.srt` subtitle file for YouTube upload.
